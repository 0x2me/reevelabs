Execution infrastructure for strategies that live on both sides of the venue boundary.

Reeve Labs builds Stepper, an engine that screens, executes and reconciles multi-leg crypto strategies spanning permissionless exchanges and regulated ones at the same time.

What we do

Carry and basis trades only pay when every leg is placed on time, hedged on the right venue, and closed before fees eat the edge. Doing that by hand across a DEX and a CEX means stale signals, fat-fingered hedges and no clean number at the end. Stepper replaces the spreadsheet and the one-person script.

  • ScreenFunding and basis compared across venues in one unit. Entry only on settled, sustained signal, never on a prediction or a stale average.
  • ExecuteBridge, swap, hedge and rebalance as one coordinated position, with each leg sized and sequenced by the engine.
  • ReconcilePer-leg cost, funding earned and final result attributed at close. Auditable, repeatable, the same every rotation.

How we work

  • Built with trading teamsWe work with desks and funds already running carry or basis strategies, deploying into their own infrastructure on fixed-fee engagements.
  • Non-custodial by designScoped permissions, propose-and-approve tiers and a client-owned kill switch. We never hold unrestricted keys.
  • Proven on our own book firstEvery strategy runs on our own capital before it runs on anyone else's.
